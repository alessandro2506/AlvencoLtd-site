# How Much Does a Mobile App Cost in the UK? 2026 Pricing Guide for Business Owners

If you've ever searched "how much does a mobile app cost" and come away more confused than when you started, you're not alone. Vague ranges, offshore price lists, and agency jargon make it genuinely difficult for UK business owners to budget sensibly.

This guide cuts through the noise. We'll walk you through real 2026 UK market pricing, the factors that drive cost up or down, which development route suits your budget, and what to watch out for before you sign anything.

## Why App Development Costs Vary So Dramatically

A mobile app is not a commodity. Asking "how much does an app cost?" is a bit like asking how much a building costs — the answer depends entirely on what you're building, who builds it, and where.

That said, there are consistent variables that determine where your project lands on the pricing spectrum:

- **Complexity and feature set** — A simple informational app costs a fraction of a platform with payments, user accounts, real-time data, and third-party integrations
- **Platform(s)** — iOS only, Android only, or both (cross-platform development adds cost but improves reach)
- **Design requirements** — Off-the-shelf UI components versus fully bespoke, branded experiences
- **Backend infrastructure** — Apps that require servers, databases, APIs, and admin dashboards cost significantly more
- **Ongoing maintenance and hosting** — Often overlooked, but typically 15–20% of the initial build cost annually
- **Who builds it** — UK agency, freelancer, or offshore team each carry different price points and risk profiles

Many UK businesses use hybrid models: UK-led strategy and project management with offshore execution.

## iOS vs Android vs Cross-Platform: The Cost Implications

Building natively for both iOS and Android historically meant two separate codebases — effectively doubling development time and cost.

**Cross-platform frameworks** like **React Native** and **Flutter** have matured significantly. In 2026, most UK studios offer cross-platform development that delivers 80–90% of native performance at 60–70% of the cost of building twice.

### When to Choose Native Development

- Your app is performance-critical (gaming, AR/VR, complex animations)
- You need deep hardware integration (e.g. NFC, biometrics, camera processing)
- You're targeting enterprise clients with strict platform requirements

### When Cross-Platform Makes Sense

- You need to reach both iOS and Android audiences on a controlled budget
- Speed to market is a priority
- Your app is primarily data-driven rather than graphics-intensive

For most UK SMEs launching their first app, cross-platform with React Native or Flutter is the pragmatic choice.

## Hidden Costs Most Businesses Don't Budget For

The build cost is only part of the picture. Projects regularly overrun or surprise clients because these items weren't scoped properly from the start.

### App Store Submission and Compliance

Apple's review process is notoriously thorough. Factor in time (and sometimes rework) for App Store compliance. GDPR compliance in the UK context adds legal review costs — particularly for apps handling personal data, health information, or financial transactions.

### Third-Party Services and API Costs

Most modern apps depend on external services:

- **Payment gateways** (Stripe, PayPal): transaction fees plus monthly costs
- **Mapping** (Google Maps Platform): usage-based billing that scales with users
- **Push notifications** (Firebase, OneSignal): free at low volume, paid at scale
- **Authentication** (Auth0, Firebase Auth): free tiers available, enterprise pricing varies
- **Cloud hosting** (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure): typically £200–£2,000/month depending on scale

### Post-Launch Maintenance

OS updates from Apple and Google regularly require app updates to maintain compatibility and store listings. Budget 15–20% of your initial development cost annually for maintenance, security patches, and minor feature iterations.

### Marketing and ASO (App Store Optimisation)

An app nobody finds is an expensive experiment. App Store Optimisation, paid user acquisition, and launch PR are separate line items that sit outside the development budget entirely.

## Which Development Route Is Right for Your Business?

### Freelancer

**Best for:** MVPs, very limited budgets, simple utilities  
**Watch out for:** Single point of failure, limited skills breadth, risk if they disappear mid-project  
**Approximate cost:** Lower end of the scale, but project management falls entirely on you

### UK Digital Studio

**Best for:** Businesses wanting end-to-end delivery, quality assurance, and accountability  
**Watch out for:** Higher day rates — but typically better documentation, IP protection, and post-launch support  
**Approximate cost:** Mid to upper range, but total project value often stronger

### Offshore Development Team

**Best for:** Larger projects where budget is the primary constraint  
**Watch out for:** Requires strong internal technical oversight; quality varies enormously  
**Approximate cost:** Significantly lower day rates, but hidden costs in management time and rework

### No-Code / Low-Code Platforms

**Best for:** Internal tools, simple workflows, proof-of-concept testing  
**Watch out for:** Scalability limits, vendor lock-in, limited customisation  
**Platforms:** Bubble, Adalo, Glide, AppSheet  
**Approximate cost:** £0–£5,000 to build; subscription costs ongoing

## How to Get an Accurate Quote

Agencies and freelancers can only quote accurately when you give them accurate information. Before approaching any developer, prepare the following:

1. **A clear problem statement** — What does this app solve, and for whom?
2. **User stories or feature list** — Prioritised into must-haves and nice-to-haves
3. **Platform preference** — iOS, Android, or both
4. **Existing systems** — Any software the app needs to connect with (CRM, ERP, POS, etc.)
5. **Target launch date** — Realistic timelines prevent rushed (and expensive) decisions
6. **Maintenance expectations** — Who handles updates and hosting after launch?

A well-prepared brief will get you more accurate quotes, faster — and signals to any agency that you're a serious client worth their best effort.

---

## Red Flags to Watch For When Getting Quotes

Not all quotes are equal. Be cautious if:

- A quote arrives with no discovery phase or scoping session
- The price seems dramatically lower than the market rates above
- There's no clear IP ownership clause in the contract
- The agency can't explain its testing or QA process
- Maintenance and hosting are not addressed
- There's no project management methodology (Agile, Scrum, etc.) defined

In the UK, ensure any agency you work with is clear on GDPR obligations, uses formal contracts, and can demonstrate a portfolio of comparable work with verifiable UK clients.
